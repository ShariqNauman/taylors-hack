EARLYEYE FOR GUARDS · MOBILE APP · AIMAN HAKIMI (GUARD B), ONE SHIFT

The guard's app runs the whole shift

Four tabs: Today, Schedule, Alerts and Me. Posts arrive from the supervisor's Plan page, the alarm takes over the screen on any tab, and every tap tells the desk where the guard is and what they found. Every screen is clickable.

+SET UP ONCEHow a guard gets the app
EarlyEye for guards An app on the guard's own phone, with its own icon. It opens full screen like any other app.
  1. Open the link the supervisor sends, for example earlyeye.app/guard
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (iPhone), or Install app (Android)
  3. Open EarlyEye from the home screen and pick your name
No app store needed for the pilot. A store version can come later with the same screens. Staff pictures and names live in the site's staff list. Only the security team sees them.
