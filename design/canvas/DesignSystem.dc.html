Daylight Desk

A light, flat, high-contrast system for a screen read from 2 m in a lit control room. Colour carries severity and nothing else. Every other signal is ink, one blue, and grey.

Principles

01Colour means severity, only severityGreen, amber and red appear only on a level. Types, status, data, brand and camera faults use ink, blue and grey.
02Loudness scales with riskCalm is pale and still. Tense is a warm fill and silent. Fight likely and fight are saturated, and they are the only states that make sound.
03Never by colour aloneEach level has a shape and a word. The four fills also step down in lightness, so the order survives greyscale and colour blindness.
04Read it from 2 mOn Live, the level, ETA, place and reason are sized for the wall. Everything else is sized for the desk at 60 cm.
05Show the number and the doubtPlaceholders, synthetic history and assumptions are dashed or hatched, never styled like a fact.
06Nothing implies video leaves the siteNo camera thumbnails on the deployed dashboard. Tiles show health and level, not frames.

  • Borders, not shadows. 1 px line by default.
  • 2 px ink or level border marks the one thing to look at: the current alert, a selected row, a decision card.
  • Dashed border: a decision, a placeholder, or synthetic data.

Motion, sound, touch

  • One motion only: a 300 ms colour change on the level pill and card.
  • Sound only at fight likely and fight. The operator arms sound once per shift, because browsers block autoplay.
  • Touch targets 44 px minimum. Confirm and Dismiss are 64 px on Live and on the phone.
  • Focus ring: 2 px Watch Blue, offset 2 px, on every control.

Shapes and icons

Level shapes are filled. Type and UI icons are 2 px strokes, Lucide-compatible at 16, 20 and 24.

Cameras
  • Content max width 1600 px. Gutters 24 px, 16 px on a phone.
  • 1280 px and up: wall or desk. Live splits 3 : 2.
  • Under about 1040 px the split wraps. The alert card stays first.
  • Under 768 px: one column, header wraps, wide tables scroll inside their box.

Words

  • Site, zone, operator, person on duty. Never student or school in the chrome; zone names come from the site table.
  • Reason is one sentence about bodies and distances, never about intent.
  • Action starts with a verb and names one person.
  • Estimates carry their assumption on the same screen.
  • Times are MYT, 24-hour, in mono: 14:31:06.
