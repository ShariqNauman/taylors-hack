V2/01

What the three competitors do

Ambient.ai

ENTERPRISE · CAMPUS
  • Monochrome: black, charcoal, white. No brand colour.
  • Condensed uppercase headlines. Mono section codes like A/02.
  • Thin vertical grid lines. Corner-bracket buttons, like a camera HUD.
  • Product shown as a dark video wall with detections on the feeds.
  • Message: an always-on reasoning layer that turns reaction into prevention.

Volt

SCHOOLS · CAMPUSES
  • Light and calm: pale blue-grey ground, white rounded cards, one blue button.
  • Alert cards in plain words: what, where, which floor, who is on the way.
  • "Verified by the review team" chip on every alert.
  • Trust chips: human-verified, no facial recognition, works with your cameras.
  • Fights framed as "noticed as they start", so staff step in early.

ZeroEyes

WEAPONS · SCHOOLS
  • Near-black pages over real camera footage.
  • Big white grotesque headline: detect threats before they escalate.
  • Four-step flow: analyse, detect, verify, dispatch.
  • Every alert human-verified within seconds, by their own staff.
  • One blue button. Red is kept for the threat itself.
V2/02

What we take

  1. Evidence first. The alert opens on the camera view with skeletons, a box, the distance line and the wrist trail drawn on it.
  2. Dark console for the product, like Ambient's video wall and ZeroEyes.
  3. Camera wall of six tiles, with the active one outlined in its level colour.
  4. Plain-words card from Volt: what, where, who is going, who confirmed.
  5. Trust chips on every screen.
  6. A four-step strip: detect, explain, push, confirm.
  7. HUD details: corner brackets, mono camera codes, REC dot, thin grid.
V2/03

What we do not take

  1. Real video on the public link. It would break the promise that video stays on site.
  2. A paid review team. Volt and ZeroEyes verify with staff. Our check is the operator's Confirm, and we say so.
  3. Faces or identity anywhere in the pipeline.
  4. Broadcast alerts. One person is pushed, never a group.
  5. Weapon claims. We detect physical aggression and crowding only.
V2/04

Evidence without breaking the privacy promise

The same incident has two views. At the desk on site, the operator sees the real recording with skeletons drawn over it. Anywhere else, people see a skeleton replay rebuilt from keypoints. No pixels leave the site.
