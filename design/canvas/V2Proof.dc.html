We claim

  • Physical aggression and crowding, from body pose.
  • A warning before the shove on our 5 escalation clips.

We do not claim

  • Verbal or online bullying.
  • Night or IR performance.
  • Weapons. That is a different product.
  • That we would have prevented any specific past case.

Model card

INPUTS
17 COCO keypoints per person from YOLO26n-pose, torso-scaled. No faces, no identities.
WINDOWS
1 s windows, stride 0.5 s. Pairwise features for any pair of people, not tracked IDs.
CLASSIFIER
Random forest or LightGBM. Types are rules on top of the score.
SPLIT
GroupKFold by video. Clip-level precision, recall and AUC. Calibrated.
THRESHOLD
Tuned on our venue footage for 1 false alarm or fewer per camera-hour. 1 s persistence.
DATA
RLVS and AIRTLab for training. CCTV-angle sets held out. RWF-2000 withdrawn, not used.
FAILS ON
Occlusion. Low light and IR. Sports play outside sports zones. Tracker ID swaps, which is why we score pairs.
