What we claim

  • Physical aggression and crowding, read from body pose.
  • A warning before the shove on our 5 escalation clips, with the lead time above.
  • False alarms per camera-hour, measured on our own normal footage.
  • Skeletons only. Video stays on the edge laptop; only the event record goes up.

What we do not claim

  • Verbal or online bullying.
  • Night or IR performance.
  • That we would have prevented any specific past case.
  • Who anyone is. There are no faces in the pipeline.

Model card

Inputs
17 COCO keypoints per person from YOLO26n-pose, torso-scaled. No faces, no identities.
Windows
1 s windows, stride 0.5 s. Pairwise features: distance, approach speed, wrist speed, arm height. Any pair of people, not tracked IDs.
Classifier
Random forest or LightGBM on window features. Types are rules on top of the score, not a model.
Split
GroupKFold by video. Clip-level precision, recall and AUC. Calibrated.
Threshold
Tuned on our venue footage for 1 false alarm or fewer per camera-hour. 1 s persistence. Tense is silent.
Data
RLVS, AIRTLab, Surveillance Camera Fight. RWF-2000 was withdrawn and is not used.
Fails on
Occlusion of one person. Low light and IR, untested. Sports play outside sports zones. Tracker ID swaps mid-fight, which is why we score pairs.
