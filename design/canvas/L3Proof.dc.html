Known weaknesses

  1. One feature carries 47% of the gain. Arm-only fights get missed.
  2. Pair features are missing 24% to 77% of the time when only one person is tracked.
  3. No own or CCTV clips. No hug-against-fight evidence.
  4. Live thresholds were tuned in simulation on RLVS, not on a webcam.
  5. 0.876 is the best of 20 settings on the same folds, so slightly optimistic.

We claim

  • Physical fights from body pose, 0.876 clip AUC on held-out public clips.
  • 12 body keypoints per person. No face points, no identities.
  • The site's video stays on site.

We do not claim

  • Early warning before a shove, until lead time is measured.
  • Any live-room accuracy or false-alarm rate yet.
  • Verbal or online bullying. Night or IR. Weapons.

Model card

POSE
YOLO26n-pose with ByteTrack. 12 body keypoints, face points dropped. About 65 pose fps on an Intel iGPU via OpenVINO.
WINDOWS
Scored every 0.5 s. 1 s and 2 s feature windows, resampled to 10 fps. The active pair is the two people whose wrists move fastest.
CLASSIFIER
LightGBM, 10 features, 15 leaves, 400 trees, learning rate 0.02.
SPLIT
5-fold GroupKFold by video. Clip score is the highest window score.
LIVE LEVELS
Calm, tense at 0.5, fight at 0.55 held for 1 s. Fight likely, ETA, type and reason are not built in the live code yet.
DATA
RLVS, 300 fight and 300 calm clips. RWF-2000 withdrawn and not used.
