Roles inside every site

Ordered by how fast they must act. The first two decide in seconds; the next two decide in minutes.
LIVE · SECONDS Primary

Desk operator

Notice the one screen that matters and send the right person.

Context
Seated at a wall of 30+ feeds. Reads our screen from up to 2 m. Eight to twelve hour shift.
Needs
One alarm. One sentence of reason. One named person to send.
Screen
Live
Success
Seconds from amber to a person sent.
Guardrail
Tense never sounds. The alarm starts at fight likely.
PHONE · 10 S Primary

Person on duty

Get there fast and calm it down.

Context
On foot with a phone, about 40 m away, one hand often busy.
Needs
Where, what and how urgent at a glance. One tap to answer.
Screen
Push
Success
Response time. Three pushes or fewer per shift, so they keep trusting it.
Guardrail
One person per alert, never a broadcast.
WEEKLY · 10 MIN Primary

Security or site manager

Put a few people where incidents cluster and keep every camera working.

Context
At a desk on Monday, with a fixed headcount and a roster to fill.
Needs
A patrol plan with its assumption stated. Hotspot evidence. Camera health. Label counts.
Screens
Plan, Incidents
Success
Incidents avoided per patrol hour. No camera black for more than a day.
Guardrail
Avoided counts are estimates. The assumption sits next to every number.
QUARTERLY Primary

Buyer or management

Approve the spend and the privacy review, then defend both.

Context
Answers to a board, parents or a regulator. Reads a summary, rarely the live screen.
Needs
Incidents avoided, staff hours, a duty-of-care record, and a privacy story: skeletons only, video stays on site.
Screens
Proof, Plan summary
Success
Renewal. Cost against one guard post.
Guardrail
Dataset and live-room numbers always side by side.
ONCE · 3 MIN Secondary

Evaluator

Decide whether this is real.

Who
Hackathon judges, a pilot sponsor, the IT or privacy reviewer.
Context
Opens the deployed link on a phone, with no camera attached.
Needs
It runs on its own. Honest numbers. Stated limits.
Screens
Live with replay, Proof
Success
Sees detect, categorize, prioritize and explain in one pass.
