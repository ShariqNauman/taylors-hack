# Campus 3D model: what was seen, and where

The diorama draws only what these sources confirm. Layout and footprints come from OpenStreetMap; floor counts from Taylor's own facilities page; shapes, colours and signature details from the photo and the architect's site plan listed below. **Google Earth 3D and Street View were not reachable from the build session** (no browser available), so anything only they could confirm is marked "not confirmed" and is modelled plainly or left out. Kayla: check this table against Google Earth and tell me what to change.

Sources used (viewed only, nothing copied into the repo):
- **OSM** footprints, campus boundary, lake, pitches, roads, car parks and lawns (ways 210350320, 210350182, 210350323, 210350316, 210350324, 210350284, 210350298, 666437569, 210350312, 210350293, 210350299, plus the highway/parking/grass ways inside the boundary).
- **Taylor's facilities page** https://university.taylors.edu.my/en/study/campus-facilities.html (which level holds what, per block).
- **Photo** "Taylors Lakeside Campus" on Wikimedia Commons (File:Taylors_Lakeside_Campus.jpg), taken from the south end of the lake looking north: Syopz Mall and U Residence on the left, Block A/B across the far end, Blocks C–E on the right.
- **Architect's existing-site plan** from KIRK's Lakeside masterplan page (kirk.studio): confirms the A/B mass, the three C/D/E fingers with a spine on the lake side, the lake stair, the entrance roundabout and the sports centre with its small pond. Its renderings show a *future* scheme and were not used for the model.
